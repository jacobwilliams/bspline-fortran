private pure subroutine db3ink_multi(x, nx, y, ny, z, nz, fcn, kx, ky, kz, iknot, tx, ty, tz, bcoef, iflag)

Multiple-output version of db3ink: interpolates nf functions on the same grid, with the same knots. The last dimension of fcn and bcoef is the output index. Evaluate them with db3val (multiple-output version).
