private pure subroutine db1ink_multi(x, nx, fcn, kx, iknot, tx, bcoef, iflag)

Multiple-output version of db1ink: interpolates nf functions on the same grid, with the same knots. The last dimension of fcn and bcoef is the output index. Evaluate them with db1val (multiple-output version).
